Yayin Studyo

Yayin Studyo is an internal publishing tool used by Karayel Games. It uploads short videos that we produce to our own YouTube, Instagram and Facebook channels at scheduled times.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it only acts on channels owned by Karayel Games after the channel owner has granted permission through each platform's official sign-in screen.
