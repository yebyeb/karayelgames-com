Privacy policy

Yayin Studyo · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Yayin Studyo ("the app") is an internal tool operated by Karayel Games. It schedules and uploads videos that we produce to social media channels that we own. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

Who can use the app

The app is not offered to the public. It runs on a computer controlled by Karayel Games and is used only with YouTube, Instagram and Facebook accounts owned by Karayel Games.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the account owner's permission, the app uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos and the youtube.readonly scope to read the channel's name and its public statistics (subscriber and view counts).
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): with the account owner's permission, the app publishes Reels to our own Pages and Instagram professional accounts and reads their follower counts.

The app does not access viewers' personal data, private messages, contacts, or any account that is not ours.

How the information is stored

Access tokens issued by Google and Meta are stored only on the computer that runs the app, in the operating system user's profile folder. They are not sent to Karayel Games servers or to any third party, and are used only to perform the uploads and statistics reads described above.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information obtained through Google or Meta APIs. Video files are sent only to the platform they are published on.

Google API Services

Yayin Studyo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used in accordance with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app itself, or from Google account permissions and from the "Business integrations" settings of Facebook. Removing access deletes the stored token for that channel.
